# Florestia Brand Direction

## Core Feeling

Cozy Amazon farming game with a playful market-management edge. The brand should feel handmade, tropical, readable, and warm rather than corporate or polished-flat.

## Mascot

- Two scarlet macaws as the main mascots.
- Use them as a pair when possible: one taller/confident, one smaller/curious.
- Keep the black pixel-outline language so they read clearly over jungle and UI backgrounds.
- Good mascot poses: perched over the logo, flying as a loading/icon motif, head close-up for alerts or dialogue.

## Logo Language

- Main wordmark: carved wood letters with chunky readable shapes.
- Secondary details: vines, leaves, small tropical flowers, and a simple farm tool accent.
- The logo should work with and without the macaws.
- Prioritize readability of `Florestia` over decorative foliage.

## Palette

- Macaw red: `#D9281E`
- Feather gold: `#F5B91E`
- River blue: `#1978B8`
- Rainforest green: `#3D8C2E`
- Deep leaf green: `#1E5B25`
- Acai purple: `#3B245D`
- Cacao gold: `#D98914`
- Soil brown: `#8A633A`
- Dark wood: `#4D2F19`
- Sky blue: `#3F7FA3`
- Cream highlight: `#EBDAB2`

## UI Direction

- Buttons: carved wood panels with lighter hover/active states.
- Icons: coin, leaf-heart stamina, sun/day, clock-moon warning.
- Terrain and crop art should share the same pixel-art outline density as the macaws.
- Avoid sterile flat UI; use subtle wood, soil, leaf, and woven-basket textures.

## Game Motifs

- Crops: mandioca, cacao, acai.
- Market: baskets, stalls, rustic signs, harvest crates.
- World: river, rainforest, farm plots, small wooden buildings.

## Expanded V2 Ideas

- Add mascot expression states for dialogue and feedback: happy, curious, surprised, proud, sleepy, confused.
- Create three logo lockups: full horizontal wordmark, compact badge with macaws, and small app icon crop.
- Use crop medallions as quick visual identifiers for mandioca, cacao, and acai.
- Add banners/flags for market categories, buyer tiers, or crop families.
- Use the macaw pair as a recurring UI personality layer: one can react to farming progress, the other can react to market choices.
- Keep environment thumbnails focused on farm, riverside market, rainforest path, and small wooden homes.
